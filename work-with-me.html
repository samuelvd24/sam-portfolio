questions people ask

We're awkward in front of cameras. Is that a problem?

You're in the majority, and no. Most of my direction is just giving you something real to do together. Ten minutes in, people forget I'm there — that's when the good photos happen.

Do you only shoot in New York?

NYC is home and where most of my work happens — Central Park elopements especially have my heart. But I'm happy to travel; tell me where your story is happening and we'll figure it out.

How many photos do we get, and when?

A preview within 48 hours, and your full gallery within a few weeks. Session galleries typically run 75+ images; elopements considerably more, depending on coverage.

What is "natural bright" editing?

Clean, warm, and true to life — real skin tones, natural light, nothing heavy-handed. The goal is photos that look like the day felt, and still will in twenty years.

How do we book?

Send an inquiry with your date if you have one. A signed agreement and retainer hold your date; the rest is due before the session.
