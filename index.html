hi, i'm sam

I photograph the in-between.

The laugh mid-sentence. The hand reaching for yours on a crowded subway platform. The deep breath before you say the thing out loud.

I'm a photographer based in New York City, and I care less about perfect and more about true. My editing style is what I call natural bright — clean, warm, real skin tones, and as much natural light as the day will give us. No trends that will look strange in ten years. Just you, as you are, on a day worth keeping.

New York is home, but I'll follow a good story anywhere.
